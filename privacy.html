Privacy — bredaz.com

This page explains how the bredaz.com website handles information. The EncryptIA app has its own privacy policy.

Last updated: 2026-10-09 Website only · app policy is separate No analytics, no tracking cookies Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires, Argentina

1 What this website collects

Nothing. The bredaz.com website has no contact forms, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels, and sets no cookies of its own. Email links simply open your mail client.

2 Information you send by email

If you write to an @bredaz.com address, we receive your email address and message and keep them for as long as needed to answer and to keep a record of the conversation. Mail is handled by Zoho Mail. We do not sell or share it, except where legally required.

3 Hosting and technical logs

The site is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and Cloudflare (DNS and CDN). Those providers may automatically log IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamp for security, performance and abuse prevention. We do not receive those logs as personal profiles and do not use them for marketing. Retention follows each provider's own policy.

4 Cookies

No cookies are set by our code. The CDN may set strictly necessary security cookies to protect against abuse; the site works with cookies disabled.

5 Third-party links and services

  • Related app: EncryptIA privacy policy (covers the mobile and web app).
  • Download and profile links point to third parties (Google Play, GitHub, Instagram, X, TikTok) whose own privacy policies apply once you leave our site.

6 Children

This website is not directed at children under 13. EncryptIA is educational software for general audiences, including the scout movement.

7 Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you sent us. In Argentina this is covered by Ley 25.326 de Protección de Datos Personales. Write to support@bredaz.com and we will respond within 10 business days.

8 Changes to this policy

We will update this page when practices change, and adjust the date below. The Terms of Service cover use of the site and our products.

9 Contact

Questions about this policy or about data protection: support@bredaz.com. Other channels are listed on the Contact page.
