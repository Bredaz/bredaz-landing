# About Bredaz — Software studio in Buenos Aires

> Canonical HTML: https://bredaz.com/about.html

1 Who we are

Bredaz is an independent software studio based in Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires, Argentina. We design and build our own products as well as custom software, combining classical engineering methods with applied artificial intelligence.

Our guiding idea is simple: software with a real purpose — tools that solve concrete, everyday problems instead of technical demos.

  * **Juan Francisco Ginabreda** — Co-founder. Product and operations.

GitHub organization: [github.com/Bredaz](https://github.com/Bredaz)

## 3 What we build

**EncryptIA** (flagship product) — teaches 40+ classical encryption methods, lets groups exchange encrypted messages, and uses AI to identify which cipher and key were used on a given ciphertext. Free on Google Play, the App Store and the web. It was born inside the scout movement.

**Custom software** — web and mobile applications, AI/LLM integration, automations and internal tools for teams and organizations.

## 4 How we work

Small team, direct contact, short iteration cycles. We scope in writing before writing code, prototype early and ship to real users.

Typical engagements: a web or mobile product from scratch, adding AI to an existing workflow, or automating an internal process.

We work in Spanish and English, remotely and in person in Buenos Aires. Pricing is quoted per project — we do not publish public price tiers.
